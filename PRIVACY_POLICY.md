# Privacy Policy for TacoScout

**Last updated: March 29, 2026**

## Overview

TacoScout ("the app") is a free iOS application that helps you find taco restaurants near your current location. This privacy policy explains what data is used, how it is used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data
TacoScout requests access to your device location while the app is in use. Your location is used solely to search for nearby taco restaurants via the Google Places API. Location data is not stored, logged, or shared by TacoScout beyond this purpose.

### Device Identifiers (Advertising)
TacoScout displays ads provided by Google AdMob. With your permission (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads. If you decline tracking, non-personalized ads will be shown instead.

## Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, or any account information
- We do not store your location history
- We do not sell any data to third parties
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting services

## Third-Party Services

TacoScout uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

- **Google Places API** — used to search for nearby restaurants. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob** — used to serve in-app advertisements. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Data Retention

TacoScout does not retain any personal data. Favorites and settings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

TacoScout is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your Choices

- **Location**: You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings → TacoScout → Location. (You will lose access to the ability to search for nearby taco spots, though)
- **Ad Tracking**: You can change your tracking preference at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
