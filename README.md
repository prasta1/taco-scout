# 🌮 TacoScout

**Because "where should we get tacos?" has haunted humanity for too long.**

TacoScout is a free iOS app that uses your location to hunt down the nearest tacos — restaurants, food trucks, holes-in-the-wall, and glorious little taquerias. Live map. Real ratings. Zero tolerance for mediocre salsa.

---

## 🔥 Features

**📍 Find tacos, fast**
Open the app. See every taco spot nearby on a live map. Tap a pin for photos, ratings, hours, address, and price level. No 14-step checkout flow. No "sign in to continue." Just tacos.

**🎚️ Filter like a taco snob**
Sort by distance, rating, or price. Show only open-now spots. Set a max search radius so you don't accidentally get suggestions 40 miles away. Your preferences stick around — because re-tapping filters is for amateurs.

**🎲 Feeling indecisive?**
Hit **Lucky Pick** and let TacoScout choose for you. It's like Russian roulette, but with carne asada. (Statistically safer.)

**🚗 Get there your way**
Fire up turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps, or summon tacos to your door via DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub — all from inside the app. Couch-mode fully supported.

**❤️ Save your favorites**
Found a legend? Heart it. Your favorites list remembers so your tastebuds don't have to.

**🏠 Home screen widget**
Glance at nearby taco spots without even opening the app. Because sometimes the craving hits mid-scroll.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

TacoScout uses your location only while the app is open, and only to find tacos. We don't store it. We don't share it. We don't sell it. We're here for the tacos, not your data. See the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
