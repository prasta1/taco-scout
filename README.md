# TacoScout

Find the best taco spots near you — instantly.

TacoScout is a free iOS app that uses your location to surface nearby taco restaurants, food trucks, and taquerias. Browse on a live map, filter by rating or price, and get there fast with built-in directions and delivery links.

---

## Features

**Find tacos fast**
Open the app and see every taco spot nearby on a live map. Tap any pin for photos, ratings, hours, address, and price level.

**Filter your way**
Sort by distance, rating, or price. Filter to open-now spots only. Set a max search radius. Your preferences are saved automatically.

**Feeling indecisive?**
Hit Lucky Pick and let TacoScout choose for you.

**Get there your way**
Launch turn-by-turn directions in Apple Maps, or order ahead via DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub — directly from the app.

**Save your favorites**
Heart any spot to save it to your favorites list for next time.

**Home screen widget**
Glance at nearby taco spots without even opening the app.

---

## Privacy

TacoScout uses your location only while the app is open to find nearby restaurants. Location data is never stored or shared. See the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
